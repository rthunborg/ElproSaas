## Findings

### Acceptance and aggregate view

1. **The formal acceptance trace remains below the P0 gate.** The deterministic
   trace has 23 of 26 P0 criteria FULL and three PARTIAL, so the 100% P0 gate
   fails. The two remediation commits added no product or test change in the
   second iteration. The affected criteria are `13.4-AC6`, `13.4-AC7`, and
   `13.4-AC8`. **Disposition: fix now.** Source:
   `_bmad-output/test-artifacts/traceability/epic-13-traceability-report.md`,
   “P0 partial coverage” and “GATE DECISION: FAIL”; also
   `_bmad-output/test-artifacts/gate-decision.json`.

2. **The server accepts a mark-sent request with no recipient selection.**
   `validateMarkQuoteVersionSent` validates recipient fields only when either
   field is present. The command then selects the legacy
   `mark_quote_version_sent` RPC when both are omitted, finalizing the quote
   without the required delivery artifact or outbox row. A browser `required`
   control does not protect a direct server-action request. This contradicts
   Story 13.4's required selected linked recipient and atomic finalization plus
   queue creation. **Disposition: fix now.** Sources:
   `src/server/commands/quotes/validation.ts:252-275`,
   `src/server/commands/quotes/mark-sent.ts:212-221`, and
   `spec-13-4-email-sending-activation.md:35,53-54,77`.

3. **Recipient correction is not implemented.** The initial recipient is
   selected, normalized, and frozen, but no command, RPC, route, migration
   transition, or test cancels a pending delivery and requires a newly
   authorized replacement after a recipient change. The existing integration
   test only proves that later CRM edits do not rewrite the frozen snapshot.
   **Disposition: fix now.** Sources:
   `spec-13-4-email-sending-activation.md:39,53-54,89`,
   `supabase/migrations/20260924110000_quote_email_delivery_artifacts.sql:125-135`,
   and `tests/integration/email/quote-delivery-recipient-snapshot.int.test.ts:54-83`.

4. **The final claimed-send quote-state guard lacks a persisted-boundary
   regression test.** The implementation does revalidate the quote and
   artifact before calling the provider: `processEmailOutbox` loads the claimed
   attachment immediately before delivery, and the SQL requires a sent quote
   with a matching fingerprint. Existing terminal-state tests cover the
   producer/reminder seam or a mock instead. This corrects the trace report's
   stronger claim that the guard is absent; the evidence gap is the missing
   regression test at the claimed outbox boundary. **Disposition: fix now.**
   Sources: `src/server/email/outbox.ts:58-70,103-110`,
   `supabase/migrations/20260924110000_quote_email_delivery_artifacts.sql:71-81`,
   `tests/integration/email/email-delivery-activation.atdd.int.test.ts:29-42,87-97`,
   and `traceability/epic-13-traceability-report.md` (`13.4-AC7`).

5. **Recovery state and recovery audit evidence are absent for quote-delivery
   preparation/finalization failures.** The schema enumerates `orphaned` and
   `invalidated` artifact states, but current transitions only consume a
   successful artifact. Failure tests assert a draft quote and zero durable
   outbox, artifact, and event rows, which cannot prove the required durable
   recovery truth. **Disposition: fix now.** Sources:
   `supabase/migrations/20260924110000_quote_email_delivery_artifacts.sql:3-14,41-50`,
   `supabase/migrations/20260924120000_email_delivery_followup_fixes.sql:13-21`,
   `tests/integration/email/quote-delivery-finalization-failure.int.test.ts:85-119,125-158`,
   and `spec-13-4-email-sending-activation.md:91`.

6. **The exposed quote-delivery email preference cannot suppress an external
   customer/contact recipient.** The suppression function compares an outbox
   recipient hash with a hash of `notification_preferences.user_id`. Quote
   delivery instead stores a hash of the selected customer or contact email.
   The preference UI presents an email toggle for the `quote.delivery`
   category, but no external recipient can satisfy that user-ID comparison.
   **Disposition: fix now.** Sources:
   `supabase/migrations/20260924090000_email_sending_activation.sql:77-86`,
   `supabase/migrations/20260924110000_quote_email_delivery_artifacts.sql:114-126`,
   and `src/components/notifications/NotificationPreferences.tsx:108-112`.

### Architectural and pattern observations

The 136-file change maintains the established server-command and RLS
boundaries: the runner endpoint, provider adapter, quote artifact seam, and
public unsubscribe route are separated. The broad diff introduces no supported
import-cycle or client-side service-role finding. Large changed test and
workflow artifacts were inspected as change-volume candidates; the evidence
does not support calling any of them a god class.

The notable process pattern is that acceptance mapping caught three P0
branches, but review of the integrated direct-action and preference paths
found two additional boundary defects. The final trace should be refreshed
after the five action items land, with its AC7 narrative updated to distinguish
implemented behavior from missing regression coverage.
