### Key Insights And Patterns

Bob (Scrum Master): "Here's what stood out across all four stories, both review tiers, and the previous-retro follow-through."

1. **The hollow-assertion / non-vacuous-coverage failure mode recurred THREE times within a single epic** (10.2's two skip/coverage-claim mismatches; 10.4's tautological `Array.isArray` cross-tenant proof) — the team correctly diagnosed mid-epic that this needs a MECHANICAL gate in dev-story finalize, not more reviewer discipline, but did not build it. This is the same class of gap as Epic 9's stale-skip-banner lint (now three epics running, 8/9/10, all named, none built).
2. **The Tier-B whole-epic chunked review, run for the first time this epic, is proven load-bearing on its very first outing** — it found 2 genuine cross-story Highs (the money-leaf source; the orphaned-escalation defect) that no per-story Tier-A pass could have reached, continuing the now five-epic-running pattern (7, 8, 9×2, 10×2) where a dedicated whole-epic lens catches what nothing else does.
3. **A NEW failure shape appeared this epic: a review-fix commit can itself be wrong or incomplete, and only a SECOND review pass over the fix — not just the original diff — catches it.** Iteration 2's banner-dismissal and terminal-status findings are both defects introduced or left incomplete BY iteration-1 fixes. This is distinct from Epic 9's "self-documented-but-unfixed gap" — here the gap was neither self-documented nor unfixed; it was fixed, and the fix was subtly broken.
4. **The manifest's "never derived==derived" grounding discipline (10.1) is the clean converse of the authored-copy-drift theme Epic 9's retro named** — where Epic 9 found four instances of copies that COULD silently diverge, Epic 10's first story structurally prevented that shape from recurring for the four scope-truth copies it collapsed, by insisting every equality proof compare against an independent ground truth.
5. **Cross-story coupling residuals were handled with documented, owner-visible non-atomicity rather than premature RPC-widening** — the auto-complete-on-lost seam (10.3) deliberately kept the frozen, immutability-critical `mark_quote_version_lost` RPC untouched and accepted a narrow, recoverable two-command race instead. This is a pattern worth naming as a positive precedent for future cross-story orchestration under Phase B's manifest-governed activation model.
6. **A precedent-setting artifact (the first read-model, the first entitlement contract) concentrates review stakes disproportionately to its own story's size** — 10.4 is the smallest story by task count but carried the epic's most consequential Tier-B finding (the money-leaf source) precisely because every later Phase B read-model inherits its shape uncorrected. Precedent-setting stories may warrant extra review weight going forward, not the same weight as a same-sized non-precedent story.
7. **The epic-mode DRAFT-PR convergence-unverified rule (E5f) worked exactly as designed** — one Tier-A High (10.4's tautological test) made the whole epic's PR ship DRAFT even though Tier B ultimately converged clean at 0 Critical/High. This is the first live proof of that rule under epic mode, and it produced the correct conservative outcome.
