1. **Declared product acceptance has deterministic evidence.** The three story artifacts are complete with no deferred acceptance items, while the trace matrix records 24/24 FULL requirements after `2a021bf1`. 2. **Cross-story shared test setup crossed the documented maintainability ceiling.** The test-quality assessment measures `tests/e2e/global-setup.ts` at 1,016 lines, after Epic 12 increased it from 927 lines, and recommends extracting the operator/onboarding seeds into a focused helper. This is a maintainability finding, not a demonstrated runtime, authorization, isolation, or data-integrity defect. **Disposition: defer as tracked remediation.** Source: `_bmad-output/test-artifacts/test-reviews/test-review-epic-12-platform-operator-and-onboarding.md`, “Split the now-over-limit Playwright global setup”.

3. **Story 12.3 trace labels are inconsistent.** The test-quality assessment found that Story 12.3 tests generally use priority markers and prose but lack the stable identifiers used in Stories 12.1 and 12.2. The trace is still FULL, so this affects diagnosis and trace navigation rather than coverage. **Disposition: defer as tracked remediation.** Source: `_bmad-output/test-artifacts/test-reviews/test-review-epic-12-platform-operator-and-onboarding.md`, “Give Story 12.3 tests stable IDs”.

4. **Production-enablement evidence remains advisory and incomplete.** The NFR assessment records no proven critical or high-severity production defect, but lacks hosted key-rollout/rotation, TLS/encryption-at-rest, perimeter, deployment-operation, capacity, recovery, telemetry, and numeric performance evidence. 5. **The only machine acceptance blocker is lifecycle tracking.** The explicit sprint-status gate reports all three Epic 12 story keys as pending because they are `review`, which forces a rejected verdict under the retrospective contract. The story artifacts' `followup_review_recommended: true` fields identify the missing independent cross-model evidence. **Disposition: fix now in the normal review lifecycle; do not change story status in this retrospective.** Source: `sprint_status.py detect-epic --epic 12`; `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/sprint-status.yaml`; all three `spec-12-*.md` frontmatters.
