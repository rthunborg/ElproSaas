## Story

As a pilot operator,
I want fallback, cutover, and sign-off decisions documented,
so that real pilot use starts only when the business accepts remaining assumptions.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** selected pilot workflows, **when** readiness is reviewed, **then** a fallback/cutover runbook documents **old-app fallback**, a **cutover-by-workflow plan**, **manual-backfill risks**, and **rollback decision points** — per workflow (cutover and rollback are per-workflow, never whole-company). [Source: epics.md#Story 9.4 AC1; architecture.md#16 (cutover-by-workflow, Lovable-fallback-until-gates-pass); PRD FR58/FR59/NFR21; test-design-epic-9.md 9.4-FALLBACK-01, R-908]
2. **Given** owner/accounting/legal questions remain, **when** the sign-off register is reviewed, **then** **quote numbering, sent-event semantics, acceptance channels, adjusted-price policy, required files, VAT, ROT, grön teknik, rounding, quote terms, and tax wording** are each clearly marked **signed-off** or **blocking real pilot use** — and each blocking item traces to its owning question ID in `owner-signoff-questions.md` (the single system-of-record). [Source: epics.md#Story 9.4 AC2; owner-signoff-questions.md; architecture.md#24 (Open Architecture Questions); test-design-epic-9.md 9.4-REG-01, R-917]
3. **Given** a blocking assumption is unresolved, **when** someone tries to approve real-pilot cutover for the affected workflow, **then** an executable **sign-off checklist validator BLOCKS pilot cutover** for that workflow (and blocks removing its fallback) — while the **demo track stays explicitly non-blocking** (demo-data-only owner decision 2026-07-03). [Source: epics.md#Story 9.4 AC3 + Stop Conditions; test-design-epic-9.md 9.4-BLOCK-01, R-905/R-908, Non-Negotiable Requirements; MEMORY (demo-data-only, tax sign-off deferred)]

**Non-AC hard constraints (epic blockers):**

- **Cutover-block is the epic-blocker teeth (R-905/R-908, 9.4-BLOCK-01).** The checklist validator MUST hard-block real-pilot cutover for ANY workflow with an unresolved blocking money/tax/immutability/acceptance/required-file/migration-classification item — a seeded open blocking item MUST fail the validator; the demo track MUST stay unblocked. This is the epic's OPS/BUS gate; it is not a passive doc. [Source: test-design-epic-9.md 9.4-BLOCK-01, Non-Negotiable Requirements; R-905/R-908]
- **Register must not drift from the system-of-record (R-917, 9.4-REG-01).** Every blocking question ID in `owner-signoff-questions.md` MUST appear in the register with a decision status; two lists disagreeing on what is blocking is the R-917 failure. The register REFERENCES `owner-signoff-questions.md`; it does NOT fork a divergent copy. Do NOT modify `owner-signoff-questions.md` itself (it is the upstream source — 9.4 consumes it). [Source: test-design-epic-9.md 9.4-REG-01, R-917; epics.md#Story 9.4 Dependencies]
- **Zero real PII in any committed doc/log/prompt (epic blocker, R-901/R-902).** Redacted/synthetic examples ONLY in `docs/migration/**`; a single real personnummer/orgnr/email/phone/address/secret is a full privacy breach. Illustrative personnummer/orgnr placeholders use obviously-fake masked forms (`YYYYMMDD-XXXX`, `XXXXXX-XXXX`) kept non-10-digit or clearly masked (the standing bare-10-digit orgnr scan, R-914).
