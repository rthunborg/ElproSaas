# Story 9.2: Anonymized Lovable Fixture Capture

Status: done

<!-- Note: Validation is optional. Run validate-create-story for quality check before dev-story. -->

## Story

As a pilot operator,
I want anonymized Lovable oracle fixtures,
so that new behavior can be tested against representative legacy examples without leaking customer data.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** selected Lovable examples, **when** fixtures are captured, **then** they **preserve business shape** for CRM, settings/pricing, calculations, quotes, PDFs, acceptance, files, and accepted-quote→job behavior, **and** real names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, personnummer, organization numbers, secrets, and raw customer files are **removed or replaced unless explicitly approved** (an approved exception carries an explicit machine-readable marker so "unless explicitly approved" is auditable, not implicit). [Source: epics.md#Story 9.2; PRD FR56/NFR17; architecture.md#17; test-design-epic-9.md 9.2-SHAPE-01, 9.2-PRIV-02, R-901/R-911]
2. **Given** fixture capture scripts are introduced, **when** they run, **then** they are **local/test-oriented, documented, repeatable, and do not require global/system changes** (a captured fixture round-trips through a lightweight golden loader, proving re-capture reproducibility). [Source: epics.md#Story 9.2 AC2; test-design-epic-9.md 9.2-REPEAT-01, R-918]
3. **Given** fixtures are committed, **when** privacy checks run, **then** they **FAIL on obvious real PII/secret patterns** — a whole-fixture-set anonymization scan (personnummer, orgnr 10-digit, non-`example.test` email, secret/password/api_key, SE phone, street-address heuristic) run over the DATA payload of EVERY committed Lovable fixture, wired into the unit gate so a seeded real PII/secret fails CI. [Source: epics.md#Story 9.2 AC3; PRD NFR17; test-design-epic-9.md 9.2-PRIV-01, R-901/R-914]

**Non-AC hard constraint (epic blocker, R-901/R-902 / 9.2-PRIV-01):** ZERO real PII — real names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, personnummer, organization numbers, secrets, or raw customer files — may appear in any committed fixture under `tests/fixtures/golden/lovable/**`, in a capture-script log/prompt, or in a capture-script committed to the repo. Anonymize AT SOURCE (the capture script never emits raw values to disk/logs — it logs counts/ids, never raw values), and the committed-fixture scan is the CI backstop. A single leaked real value is a full privacy breach regardless of numeric risk score. [Source: PRD NFR17; project-context.md#Lovable Oracle Policy; test-design-epic-9.md R-901/R-902, Non-Negotiable Requirements]
