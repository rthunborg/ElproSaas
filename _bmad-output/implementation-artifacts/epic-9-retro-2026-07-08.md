### Key Insights And Patterns

Bob (Scrum Master): "Here's what stood out across all five stories, the epic-boundary review, and the previous-retro follow-through."

1. **The epic's two executable stories (9.2, 9.3) carried the epic's real risk, and both delivered fail-closed, positive-control-proven guards — exactly as the story-level Dev Notes predicted going in.** Every privacy/representativeness/labelling guard this epic built was proven, via a seeded negative case, to actually fire. This is the epic living up to its own stated risk model.

2. **A self-documented gap is not the same as a closed gap — and this is now visible as a distinct failure shape from Epic 8's "silent carry."** Epic 8 had a silent carry (an item nobody re-mentioned). Epic 9's `registerBlockingIds()` gap was explicitly commented on IN the code by the dev who shipped it weaker than the AC required. The Tier-A reviewer correctly treated the comment as a fix candidate, not absolution — worth stating as a standing review principle.

3. **The dedicated epic-boundary review lens is now proven FOUR epics running (7, 8, and now the two iterations of 9) as the mechanism that catches the one cross-story/cross-facet defect no per-story review or CI reaches.** The `A22` dual-status conflict is this epic's instance of the same pattern Epic 7's `owner_type` mismatch and Epic 8's acceptance-evidence-upload scope-handoff gap represented. This is unambiguously load-bearing at this point, not incidental.

4. **A privacy-blocker-class guard (docs-only PII scan) has a coverage BOUNDARY that itself needs to be understood, not assumed universal.** The real-orgnr-in-test-data finding exists specifically because the whole-directory scan covers `docs/migration/**` but not `tests/unit/**` — a boundary that was correct-by-design for the docs-only stories but became a blind spot once test fixture DATA (not docs) also needed the same zero-real-PII discipline.

5. **The single-source-of-truth / authored-copy-drift risk shape appeared four times independently in one epic** (overlapping control-point doc prose, forked ID-extraction regexes, hand-copied `ReadinessCode` array, hardcoded fixture-filename enumeration) — all Low, all accurate-today, all flagged by the epic-boundary review as one theme worth ONE consolidation pass rather than four scattered fixes.

6. **The Epic-8 action item to add a stale-skip-banner lint remains unimplemented for a second epic running**, even though the underlying discipline was manually applied correctly every time in Epic 9. Manual discipline holding twice in a row is good; it is not the same as the mechanical check existing.

7. **The demo-track vs real-pilot-track separation — the central safety property this whole epic exists to protect — held with zero conflation across all five stories**, including under the executable cutover-block checklist's positive AND negative test paths. This is the epic's most consequential structural property, and it proved out cleanly.
